What this is, and isn't

      
The vibe comes from taskfm's keyword classifier: no model call, same answer every time. The artists come from Qloo's /v2/insights, seeded with your taste via /search and steered by a genre tag from /v2/tags. Artists you listed, and ones already played this session, are excluded.

      
Qloo results describe aggregate taste affinities. They are a good starting point for music, not a claim about you. Spotify links open a search; playback happens in the local CLI.
